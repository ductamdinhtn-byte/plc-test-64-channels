🧩 How to Test All Industrial Components (Relays, Switches, Solenoids, SSRs & Fuses)

Your Tester Box supplies +12V (~3–5 mA) from CH #01–64 (PC817 Sense) and switches GND (0V) on DRV #01–16 (MOSFET OUT-). Current flows directly through the component to verify both the wire crimps and the component itself.

1️⃣ Best Multi-Component Wiring: Shared Common Return Bus

[TESTER: DRV #01–08] ──► Pin 1..8 ──► [Component 1..8 (-)] ┐
 (MOSFET OUT- GND)                                          ├──► Pin 9 (Common +)
[TESTER: CH #01–08]  ◄──────────────────────────────────────┘    (+12V PC817 Sense)

Connect individual component wires (Pins 1–8) to DRV #01–#08, and connect their shared Common wire (Pin 9) to CH #01 (or jumper across CH #01–#08). The ESP32 pulses one component at a time!

2️⃣ Critical Polarity Rule (Diodes, LEDs, SSRs & Coils with Flyback Diode)

[ CH #01–64 (+12V Anode) ] ───► [ Component (+) ] ───► [ Component (-) ] ───► [ DRV #01–16 (GND Cathode) ]
  • Positive (+ / Anode / A1): Always land on CH #01–64 (MUX / PC817 Sense).
  • Negative (- / Cathode / A2): Always land on DRV #01–16 (MOSFET OUT-).
🧲 1. Relay & Contactor Coils 10 Ω – 1.5 kΩ
Coil A1 (+) → CH #01 (+12V)
Coil A2 (-) → DRV #01 (GND)

12V (~4 mA) flows directly through the copper coil winding to trigger ✅ PASSED without pulling in heavy 24V armatures. Immediately detects broken coil windings or loose A1/A2 ferrules.

🔘 2. Switches, Buttons & Contacts 0 Ω Closed
Switch COM → DRV #02 (GND)
Contact NC / NO → CH #02 / CH #03

NC (Normally Closed) shows ✅ PASSED immediately. Press the button or toggle the limit switch while watching the Live Matrix Monitor to see NO flip to ✅ PASSED!

⚙️ 3. Solenoid Valves, Fans & Heaters 15 Ω – 1.2 kΩ
Valve/Heater (+) → CH #04 (+12V)
Valve/Heater (-) → DRV #04 (GND)

Tests right through the valve solenoid or heater element via your connector harness (e.g., J15 / P20 / DB9). Verifies both pins are seated in the plug.

💡 4. Solid State Relays (SSR) & LEDs 1.2V – 3V Drop
SSR Input +3 (Anode) → CH #05 (+12V)
SSR Input -4 (Cathode) → DRV #05 (GND)

Forward-biases the internal SSR optocoupler LED! If wired backwards, the diode blocks current and shows ❌ OPEN—catching reversed polarity instantly.

🛡️ 5. Fuses, Breakers & Overloads 0 Ω Intact
Line Side (L1 / 95) → DRV #06 (GND)
Load Side (T1 / 96) → CH #06 (+12V)

Verifies main panel fuses (FU1/FU2), circuit breakers (CB1), and thermal overload NC contacts (95–96) in series with the power distribution harness.

📡 6. High-Z Analog / IC Terminals > 5 kΩ (Clip Screw)
Cable Plug Pin → DRV #07 / #08
Component Screw Head → CH #07 / #08

For 0–10V analog controllers (e.g., Watlow) that block DC when unpowered, clip CH directly onto the component's metal terminal screw head (or use a WAGO jumper) to verify metal-to-metal wire seating.

🏭 How to Test a Siemens PLC Harness & Terminal Blocks (X10 Digital Inputs & X11 Digital Outputs)

Complete wiring procedure to test all 14 Digital Inputs (DI a.0–b.5), 10 Digital Outputs (DQ a.0–b.1), and Power/Common rails (L+, M, 1M, 3L+, 3M) using your 16-MOSFET × 64-MUX Tester Box.

🔼 STEP 1: Top Terminal Block X10 (14 Digital Inputs DI a.0 – DI b.5)

  1. Method A (Harness Plug to X10 Terminal Screws): Plug the harness source into DRV #01–#14 and clip/land the 14 Siemens X10 terminal wires (DI a.0 .. DI b.5) onto CH #01–#14.
  2. Method B (Through Siemens 1M Input Optocouplers / Live PLC LEDs): Connect Siemens 1M (Input Common) to 12V GND / DRV #01 and pulse DI a.0..b.5—watch the 14 green DI LEDs on the Siemens PLC front panel light up in sequence!

Step-by-Step Operator Method Sheet (OMS) & Quality Procedure

Standard Operating Procedure for PLC Schematic Parsing, 64-Channel Tester Box Hookup & Automated Continuity Verification.

STEP 01 — Pre-Operation Safety & Power Supply Verification (01 Dashboard)

  1. Ensure the Unit Under Test (UUT) PLC harness or control panel is completely disconnected from external AC/DC line power before connecting to the 64-Channel Tester Box.
  2. Verify the 64-Channel Tester Box power distribution rails: +12V/+24V DC Source Rail, +5V/+3.3V Logic Supply, and 0V Common Ground.
  3. Open 01 Dashboard to confirm system readiness and review live pass/fail test counters.

STEP 02 — Connect 64-Channel Hardware HIL via USB Serial (02 Connect Hardware HIL)

  1. Plug the USB cable from the 38-Pin ESP32 Controller into the workstation PC (default port COM17 at 115200 bps).
  2. Go to 02 Connect Hardware HIL (or click Connect Serial Hardware in the top bar) and select the ESP32 COM port until the status indicator turns 🟢 Connected.
  3. Confirm the 16×64 hardware pin assignment: Rail A (16 MOSFET Drivers DRV #01–16): GPIOs {26, 25, 32, 33, 16, 17, 18, 19, 21, 22, 23, 4, 2, 5, 15, 3} • Rail B (64 Opto/MUX Sensors CH #01–64): Address Bus S0=13, S1=12, S2=14, S3=27 and 4× MUX SIG inputs {34, 35, 0, 39}.

STEP 03 — Upload Engineering Schematic PDF & Extract Pinout (03 Schematic Parser)

  1. Navigate to 03 Schematic Parser and click the green Upload PDF / Schematic button in Window 1 to load your PLC wiring drawing (e.g., 94-03143-04.pdf).
  2. Inspect Window 2 (Extracted Wire & Terminal Table) to verify every extracted wire ID, wire gauge (AWG), Source Terminal (Pin A), Destination Terminal (Pin B), and assigned Tester Box screws (DRV # → CH #).

STEP 04 — Review AutoCAD Vector Routing & Ladder Logic Rungs (04 CAD & Ladder)

  1. In Window 3 (AutoCAD Vector Canvas), select a standard connector preset (DB9, DB15, 4-Pin Motor, 16-Ch Direct, or Full 64-Ch Matrix) or use the uploaded schematic pinout, and add any custom pins with + Add Pin.
  2. In Window 4 (Logic Ladder Diagram), verify each rung's MOSFET Driver contact (| |) and Optocoupler Sensor coil (( )), or click ⚡ Live Probe Hardware Rungs to probe continuity directly on the ladder diagram.

STEP 05 — Verify Real-Time PLC Bill of Materials & Excel BOM Link (05 BOM)

  1. Open 05 BOM and review the ⚡ Real-Time Schematic PLC BOM tab to confirm all required PLC modules, controllers, relays, connectors, and point-to-point wire schedules match the schematic.
  2. Switch to 📚 PLC Components Library to upload component reference photos or click Import Excel BOM (.csv / .xlsx) / 🔗 Link Excel to synchronize part numbers with the master engineering BOM.

STEP 06 — Execute Continuity Test & Pinpoint Any Faults on Schematic (06 Continuity Test)

  1. Connect the UUT harness Source ends to Tester Box Rail A (DRV #01–16) and Destination ends to Tester Box Rail B (CH #01–64) following the Wire Hookup Schedule below.
  2. Click ⚡ Instant 64-Ch Scan for an immediate full-matrix check, or click ▶ Start Sequential Test to step through each wire one by one.
  3. If any discrepancy occurs (OPEN CIRCUIT, SHORT / CROSSTALK, or SWAPPED WIRE), inspect the red Hardware Fault Locator and click 🎯 Point Out & Link to Schematic to highlight the exact fault location across the Schematic, AutoCAD, and Ladder views.

STEP 07 — Final Quality Sign-Off & Export OMS / Test Certificate PDF (07 OMS Instruction)

  1. Verify that 100% of wires show PASSED in the Live Project Wire Hookup & Verification Schedule below.
  2. Enter the Operator Name and Work Order number above, then click Export / Save OMS as PDF at the top-right to generate and save the official PDF Operator Method Sheet & Inspection Record.
